# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Students, professionals, and anyone needing focused time management. They use it while working or studying to maintain rhythm and avoid burnout.

## Product Purpose
A simple, distraction-free Pomodoro timer (TomoTimers) that helps users stay on track, focusing on the core task of time-boxing.

## Brand Personality
Simple, focused, minimalist, stark, but enhanced with intentional animations to keep the experience engaging.


### Environment Variables
- `NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_ID`: Set this to your Google Analytics 4 Measurement ID (e.g., `G-XXXXXXXXXX`) to enable tracking.
## Anti-references
- Cluttered dashboards.
- Overly complex settings or menus that distract from the timer.

## Design Principles
1. **Focus First:** Remove visual noise; the timer is the hero.
2. **Obvious UX:** Controls should be self-evident and require zero learning curve.
3. **Intentional Motion:** Use animation to reward progress and indicate state changes without being distracting.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Ensure high contrast for timer text, accessible focus states for timer controls, and support for reduced motion preferences given the animation requirement.
